Process Architecture

From Biomass Residue to Industrial Fuel

A four-stage zero-binder densification process transforming high-volume agricultural residues into compact high-energy cylindrical solid fuels.

precision_manufacturing Zero Chemical Additives
01 Intake

Raw Biomass Selection

Strict screening of regional farm residue, timber shavings, and agricultural shells with under 15% inbound foreign matter.

02 Refinement

Milling & Thermal Drying

Automated pulverization into sub-5mm particles paired with hot-air rotary dehydration locks moisture firmly under 8%.

03 Extrusion

High-Pressure Densification

Continuous mechanical ram extrusion compresses particles under 1,200 kg/cm² pressure, liberating native natural lignins.

04 Quality Pass

Cooling & Dispatch Staging

Counter-flow cooling stabilization, robotic packaging into 50kg bags or bulk loose consignments with weighbridge verification.
